Main takeaway

  • Prompt conditions produce measurable TAM-derived attribution behavior changes.
  • Several prompts have distinct metric fingerprints, especially misleading, object-detection, and order-disruption prompts.
  • Some bbox/location-style outputs are discordant with diffuse TAM behavior.
  • Image structure can now be explored using joined COCO annotations.
  • These are metric-space findings, not causal faithfulness or ground-truth localization claims.
